Questions people DM

What TVs work?

If it has a browser, yes: Samsung, LG, Fire TV, Android TV boxes, Apple TV via AirPlay, or any laptop stuck to the back of the screen. No install.

Does it keep working offline?

The last weather, prices and quotes stay on the board until feeds return. Your messages never depend on the internet.

How does the Shopify part work?

You create a free custom app in your Shopify admin with read-only orders permission. Our tiny relay script turns that into the sales slide. Nothing writes to your store — ever.

Can I get it custom-branded?

Yes — venue licenses include your colors, logo plate text and themes. Email after purchase and it is turned around same day.
