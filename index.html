It runs on the screen you already own.

No stick PC, no controller, no monthly channel fee. Anything with a web browser can be a departure board, and a schedule link means your messages change by themselves while nobody is in the room.

  1. Open the link on the screen.

  2. Set your city, your words, your hours.

  3. Press F. Walk away. It keeps running.

Your phone becomes the touch screen.

Pair once, and the phone mirrors the board. Tap a flap to change that letter. Type a sentence and it writes itself across the whole board, on the TV, with the clicking and all of it.

  1. The TV shows a QR code.
  2. Scan it with any phone camera.
  3. That phone is now a touch screen and a keyboard.

One command, on any machine already on your network:

node tools/touch-relay.mjs

No account, no cloud, no port forwarding. The phone and the TV talk directly over your own wifi, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

FLAPBOARD is free and open source. Clone it, run it, put it on a wall.
